# Hey, I'm Davi 👋

I'm working to make AI a natural part of my career and daily life, so it handles the busywork and I get more time for what matters most: people. I learn by building, mostly with a homelab and local AI.

## Why

Technology should give time back. The more I can hand off to AI, the more time I have for real human connection.

## How I'm doing it

AI is the center of my own learning journey: I use it to teach me how to use AI. Along the way I'm building a private collection of local models and services for myself and my family, so we get the benefits without handing our data to anyone else.

## What I'm up to

- 🖥️ Running a homelab on Proxmox: my own private place to test ideas
- 🧠 Running local LLMs on my own hardware, keeping my data mine
- 🤖 Building AI agents and workflows that take real tasks off my plate
- 📚 Learning in public: lots of "that didn't work, here's why"
